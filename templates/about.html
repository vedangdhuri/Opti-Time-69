About The Project

Meet Opti-Time

An advanced, intelligent Academic Timetable Management System designed to eliminate the headaches of manual scheduling through automated conflict resolution and algorithmic constraint solving.

How it Works

A streamlined workflow from data entry to PDF export.

1

Input Constraints

Set up your classes, teachers, subjects, and batch divisions. Specify the precise theory and practical credit requirements according to the MSBTE curriculum guidelines.

2

Algorithmic Generation

With a single click, Opti-Time runs thousands of randomized iterations. It calculates the optimal distribution of lectures and practicals, strictly avoiding teacher overlaps across all divisions.

3

Review & Validation

Use our built-in Validation Dashboard to ensure every class has precisely the right number of theory and practical hours. Check the Teacher Workload page to prevent faculty burnout.

4

Export & Publish

Once satisfied, seamlessly export visual, color-coded timetables into high-res PNGs or PDFs, ready to print and distribute to students and faculty.

Why Opti-Time?

Cross-Class Conflict Avoidance

Most basic generators fail when a professor teaches multiple years and divisions. Opti-Time actively checks against a global registry, guaranteeing a teacher is never booked in two places at once.

Batch Management

Seamlessly schedules 2-hour consecutive practical blocks, correctly dividing classes into separate batches running simultaneous labs.

Workload Balancing

It dynamically shuffles and evaluates the schedules to distribute a teacher's lectures evenly throughout the week, isolating and fixing any timetable deficits.

Data Integrity Controls

The system is built on a robust Django backend with SQLite, ensuring class data is saved safely, forms are validated securely, and access is protected behind an authentication wrapper.
